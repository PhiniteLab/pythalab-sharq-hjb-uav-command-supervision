# Scientific progression ledger

Cumulative record of what was claimed, how it was tested, what came out, and how
belief changed as a result: claim → test → outcome → revised belief → cost.
Dead-ends and failures are first-class entries, not omissions.

Maintenance rule: every work unit appends its `P-` entries at closure.
